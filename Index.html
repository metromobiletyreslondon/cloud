What Happens During a Mobile Tyre Replacement? A mobile tyre replacement is designed to get a damaged or worn tyre changed without taking the vehicle to a garage. Instead, a fitter comes to the car, van or SUV with the tyre and equipment needed to complete the work at the vehicle’s location. The Booking Starts With Your Vehicle Details When arranging the callout, you will usually be asked for your vehicle registration, tyre size, location and a description of the problem. If you know the tyre size, it will normally look something like: 225/45 R17 Providing the correct information helps the fitter bring a suitable replacement tyre before setting off. The Fitter Arrives at Your Location The vehicle needs to be parked somewhere safe enough for the work to be carried out. This could be: At home At work In a car park On a driveway At a suitable roadside location The fitter will first inspect the affected tyre and confirm whether replacement is required. The Vehicle Is Lifted Safely Once the problem has been confirmed, the fitter uses appropriate lifting equipment to raise the vehicle and remove the affected wheel. If a locking wheel nut is fitted, the correct key will usually be needed. If the key is missing or damaged, additional removal work may be required. The Old Tyre Is Removed The wheel is placed on tyre-changing equipment so the damaged or worn tyre can be removed from the rim. The fitter can also inspect the wheel for obvious signs of damage before installing the replacement tyre. The Replacement Tyre Is Fitted The new tyre is installed onto the wheel and inflated to the correct pressure. The tyre must be seated properly on the rim before the wheel can be balanced and returned to the vehicle. The Wheel Is Balanced Wheel balancing is an important part of tyre replacement. Small balancing weights may be added to the wheel to reduce vibration when the vehicle is driven at speed. Mobile tyre vans can carry balancing equipment so this can be completed at the vehicle. The Wheel Goes Back on the Vehicle Once the tyre has been fitted and balanced, the wheel is reinstalled and secured correctly. The fitter will then lower the vehicle and carry out final checks before the job is completed. Conclusion Mobile tyre replacement follows many of the same steps as a traditional tyre shop, but the equipment comes to the vehicle instead. From removing the damaged tyre to fitting, inflating and balancing the replacement, the work can be completed where the vehicle is already parked, provided the location is safe and suitable.
